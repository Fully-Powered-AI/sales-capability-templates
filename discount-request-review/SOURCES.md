# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Four files of the deal desk method in alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT) shaped the work. From the method's main instructions in commercial/skills/deal-desk: reviewing one request at a time between the salesperson and the people who sign, sending each request to the people a policy names rather than deciding it, and asking what the customer commits in return for a deeper price. From commercial/skills/deal-desk/references/discount_economics.md: measuring a discount against the margin it removes, because the cost of delivery does not fall when the price does, and setting a request beside the discounts the company usually gives. From commercial/skills/deal-desk/references/deal_desk_canon.md: going no higher than the approval level a request lands in, showing every extra approver a rule adds, and treating a changed quote as a fresh request. From commercial/skills/deal-desk/assets/deal_intake_template.md: one written request as the record of the ask, and counting only what the customer has actually committed. Fully Powered AI built the work around the organization's own written policy as the only authority, with every clause accounted for, asks the policy does not cover named rather than ruled on, contract terms set aside for the person who handles them, the comparison with the organization's own decision log, a blank decision line for each approver, and the rule that nothing is approved, sent or recorded by the assistant. No third-party text is reused.
