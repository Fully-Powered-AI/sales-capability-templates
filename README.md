# Fully Powered Capability Templates: Sales

Answer in minutes what used to take weeks. These capability templates take the preparation that slows a sales team down and give an AI assistant a precise way to do it, with every claim traced to evidence and a person deciding what goes to the buyer.

This repository holds 13 capability templates for sales teams. Each one is a single piece of work with its own folder: what it is for and who starts it, the complete template an AI assistant follows, a worked example with invented data, what to change for your company, what has been checked, and its sources.

## The capabilities

- **[Bid or no-bid and RFP response plan](bid-or-no-bid-and-rfp-response-plan/)**: Before anyone starts on a response, your bid manager sees every requirement in the request checked against your own evidence, your bid criteria tested and the buyer questions ready. A named person decides whether to bid; then a dated plan says who does what by when.
- **[Cold outreach sequence drafting](cold-outreach-sequence-drafting/)**: Your outreach reads like you wrote it on your best day: a first note and its follow-ups, drafted in your own voice, where every line about the prospect traces to a dated fact and every line about your company traces to facts you supplied. You read it, edit it and send it yourself.
- **[Competitor battlecard](competitor-battlecard/)**: Your salespeople get one page on one named competitor: where it is strong, where you are stronger and the proof, what to say when it comes up, and the questions to ask. Nothing about the competitor goes on the card without a dated source; anything unsupported is held back for the approver.
- **[Deal close plan](deal-close-plan/)**: Your account executive holds one late-stage deal on two pages: the buyer's case for buying now, counted from the buyer's own figures, and a dated plan of every step to signature with its owner on each side. The seller decides what the buyer sees; nothing reaches the sales system without approval.
- **[Discount request review](discount-request-review/)**: Before a price below list goes to a customer, whoever approves it gets one page: the discount and what it costs in margin, every clause of your own written policy met or missed, who approves and in what order, and what the customer gives in return. A person decides; nothing is sent.
- **[Objection answers and sales one-pager](objection-answers-and-sales-one-pager/)**: Walk into the call with an answer for every objection you expect, each with its honest limit and the proof behind it, and one page to leave behind. Everything is built from the facts you supply, so nothing on the page is a claim you cannot stand behind.
- **[Prospect list by role and firm type](prospect-list-by-role-and-firm-type/)**: Before anyone writes a first note, you know which kinds of firm and which roles in them deserve your next conversations. Every firm is tested against your own criteria first and carries a sourced reason, a grade and the questions only a conversation can answer. Nobody is contacted.
- **[Sales call preparation brief](sales-call-preparation-brief/)**: Your salesperson, partner or account manager walks into every meeting with a brief they can read in five minutes: who will be there, what has been said and promised so far, what is still open, and a plan for the call. Every line traces to your own notes or a public page, and anything inferred is labelled.
- **[Sales call scorecard](sales-call-scorecard/)**: After a sales call, your sales lead sees how it went against your team's own criteria, each point backed by the words actually said, where the call left the deal, and one focus for the next call. It reviews a call, never a person, and gives no score for anyone's file.
- **[Sales pipeline review](sales-pipeline-review/)**: Each week your sales lead sees the whole open pipeline on one page: value and age by stage, what moved since last week, the deals at risk and coverage against your target. Every figure traces to your export, and the forecast number is left for a person to commit.
- **[Sales proposal preparation](sales-proposal-preparation/)**: Your account executive answers an RFP in minutes, not weeks, with a proposal draft where every claim traces to approved company information and every gap is already a question for the right person.
- **[Stalled-deal review](stalled-deal-review/)**: Your sales lead sees, from the record alone, which open deals have stopped moving, what each one's stage still lacks and whose move it is, and where every recent no ended and why. Nothing in your sales system is changed; corrections are proposed for a person to make.
- **[Win-loss review](win-loss-review/)**: Once a quarter your sales lead sees, from the deals you closed, where the losses ended, which kinds of deal you win and lose, what the largest deals show, and which findings rest on enough deals to act on. Actions are proposed in proportion to the evidence, and nothing in your sales system is changed.

## How to use one

Open a folder and start with its README. Every folder stands alone, so copy the one you want and leave the rest. The templates are written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini: paste the instruction block and the output format in as custom instructions, or load the folder in a product that reads `SKILL.md` packages.

In every template a person starts the work, supplies the inputs and decides. The template prepares and checks. Anything that sends, posts, pays or changes a record waits for a person to approve it.

## What "checked" means here

Each folder says exactly what was checked and when: who wrote it, that its sources and rights were reviewed, the date of its editorial review, and whether it has been tried on its invented example in a named AI product. Nothing is described as tested for a client unless it was. Test a template on your own past work before your team relies on it; each one comes with acceptance tests for that.
