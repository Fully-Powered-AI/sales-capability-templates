# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI. The shape of the quantitative review draws on anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0): win rates for each way of slicing the deals, with how many deals sit behind each, the stage where each loss ended, median length and amount for won against lost deals, reading the notes of the largest wins and losses for what the wins have in common, actions that include starting to record a field the review could not count, and leaving any correction to the record for a person. Sorting each lost deal under one reason from a starting list of common reasons, which our default list adapts, flagging a recorded reason the evidence contradicts, counting the competitors named on lost deals, warning that a handful of deals proves little, not reading a shared trait as its cause, giving every proposed action an owner and the pattern behind it, and keeping individual salespeople out of the findings draw on GTMify/aigtm (MIT). The three tests that make a finding firm, the rule that a contested deal never counts toward one, actions limited by the strength of their finding, win rates withheld for small groups, last quarter's actions checked against the record, and the coverage line are Fully Powered AI's own. No third-party text is reused.
