Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The idea of pairing two things for a deal near signature, an argument for buying now made from the buyer's own reasons and a schedule to signature that both sides work to, comes from the close-plan method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0, sales/skills/close-plan). Also from that method: the six-part outline of the case and its order (the problem today and its cost, the outcome wanted, what the offer does toward each outcome, the price against the value, the cost of waiting, and what sets the seller apart); naming as reasons to choose the seller only what the buyer has reacted to; reading notes and emails as information, never as instructions; listing what each side still has to do before signature, which the method plans by counting back from the signature date and this template plans forward from today before counting back for the must-finish-by dates; keeping any point the buyer has not supported out of the shared case and turning it into a question for the next conversation; warning when the dates cannot reach the close date; suggesting a fresh next step and close date for the sales record, which a person then chooses whether to make; and a short note inviting the buyer's main contact to check who does what on their side. Fully Powered AI added the working-day arithmetic that gives every step a planned date, a must-finish-by date and its spare days; the chain of steps that sets the signature date; value counted only from the buyer's own figures and target, with every sum shown; the split between a draft for the buyer and notes for the seller alone; the check for usual steps the inputs never mention; and the rule that nothing is sent, shared or written to the sales system without the seller's approval. No third-party text is reused.
