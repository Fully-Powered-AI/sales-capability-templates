# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI from its own working guide for sales conversations, which is itself a rewrite of the sales collateral method in coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT). The list of objection kinds, the question of whether a buyer wants a reason or better terms, the short statement of the concern behind an objection, and the shape of a spoken answer (start with what the buyer has right, use what the buyer has already said, one piece of proof, close on a question) draw on anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0). The wording throughout is Fully Powered AI's own.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Its own working guide for preparing sales conversations: one-pagers and objection answers with their limits.
- **[coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills)** at commit `c7d7dfb6b460fc376b4b2204b78a9c9fd5ca6467`, `skills/sales-enablement`, MIT. The method our working guide rewrote: a quick sheet beside a fuller entry for each objection, the concern behind the words and a question to settle it when it is unclear, proof a salesperson can defend, treating what a buyer reports about another supplier as unchecked, a closing question, saying when to stop arguing, keeping a record of objections after the call, a short form when time is short, and the order of a one-page leave-behind. No text is reused. Its licence notice at that commit is in [notices/coreyhaines31-marketingskills-LICENSE.txt](notices/coreyhaines31-marketingskills-LICENSE.txt).
- **[anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins)** at commit `02ffa99ff00ce4d3046ccebabb8357cb39f3ab06`, `sales/skills/handle-objection`, Apache-2.0. Four ideas: the kinds an objection can be sorted into; asking whether the buyer wants a reason to go ahead or better terms; stating the concern behind the words in a sentence or two; and the shape of a short spoken answer that starts with what the buyer has right, uses what this buyer has already said, brings one piece of proof and closes on a question. No text is reused. Its licence notice at that commit is in [notices/anthropics-knowledge-work-plugins-LICENSE.txt](notices/anthropics-knowledge-work-plugins-LICENSE.txt).
