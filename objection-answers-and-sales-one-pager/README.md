# Objection answers and sales one-pager

Walk into the call with an answer for every objection you expect, each with its honest limit and the proof behind it, and one page to leave behind. Everything is built from the facts you supply, so nothing on the page is a claim you cannot stand behind.

*Workflow template · Sales · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/objection-answers-and-sales-one-pager/)*

## The outcome

Before the call you hold a call pack: a quick sheet you can glance at while the buyer is talking, a fuller entry for each objection, and one page the buyer can pass to whoever else decides. Every salesperson on the team answers the hard questions the way your best one does.

Each answer carries its limit in the same breath, so it survives being repeated without you in the room. Where your facts do not support an answer, the pack says so and tells you what to find out. Where the buyer has found a real gap, it says when to stop arguing. Buyers check what they are told, and an answer that holds up when checked is the one that wins the next meeting.

## Who starts the work, and when

The salesperson or owner taking the call starts it, a day or two before: after a first conversation, when a buyer pushes back by email, or before a meeting where a new person joins. It also works on a single objection, when the buyer has just raised one and you speak to them again the same day.

The sales lead or owner reviews the pack against the facts and approves anything that names a customer. The salesperson sends the one-pager. The AI assistant sends nothing.

## What they supply

- **Your facts.** A short fact sheet: what you sell and to whom, prices and terms as written, what is and is not included, the proof you are allowed to share with its source and date, references with permission on record, and the next step you can offer.
- **The call.** Who will be there, their roles, the date and what happens after.
- **What the buyer has said.** Call notes and emails, in the buyer's words.
- **The objections.** The ones you have heard, word for word, and the ones you expect.
- **Who the one-pager is for.** The person on the call, or the person they will pass it to.

## What comes out

1.  **A quick sheet.** One line per objection: the answer with its limit inside it, and the proof to point to.
2.  **Objection answers.** For each one: the exact words, the worry behind them, a short spoken answer in plain language, the limit, the proof to have ready, the question to ask next and when to stop arguing.
3.  **A one-pager.** The buyer's problem in their words, what you do, what it costs, what is not included, what they can check and the next step. One page, read in under a minute. It is built only from statements already in the fact check for the answers, so the page and the conversation say the same thing.
4.  **Open items.** What to ask the buyer, what needs a colleague's answer or approval, and what is missing from your facts.
5.  **A fact check.** Every statement in the pack beside the fact it came from, marked as copied from your facts, the buyer's own words, your note of what they said, something the buyer reported about another supplier, or held for approval.

Good looks like this: you could read any answer aloud to a buyer who then checks it, and nothing in it would embarrass you.

## What has to change for your company

- **Your fact sheet.** Write it once, give it an owner and a date, and keep it current. It is the only thing the answers are built from. The assistant states your facts as written and draws no conclusions of its own, so include the plain conclusions you want your salespeople to say.
- **What proof may be shared.** Which figures, examples and customer names can go to a prospect, who approved each, and how old a figure may be before it is checked again.
- **Who approves.** The role that confirms prices, the role that releases a reference, and the role that reads the pack before it is used.
- **Your one-pager.** Its length, its headings and who may send it.
- **Where objections are kept.** A simple log of what buyers said and how it went makes the next pack better.
- **Your platform.** The instructions are written to be pasted into ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. Decide which notes and files the assistant may read for this work.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
