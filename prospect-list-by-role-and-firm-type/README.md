# Prospect list by role and firm type

Before anyone writes a first note, you know which kinds of firm and which roles in them deserve your next conversations. Every firm is tested against your own criteria first and carries a sourced reason, a grade and the questions only a conversation can answer. Nobody is contacted.

*Capability template · Sales · version 0.1.0 · [Capabilities Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/capabilities/prospect-list-by-role-and-firm-type/)*

## The outcome

Your outreach starts from a short list you can defend line by line. For each kind of firm you sell to, you see the role to reach, the person who would sponsor the work, and the firms worth your effort now, with the strongest at the top.

Every firm on the list earns its place. Your size, region and firm-type rules are checked before anything else, so a firm that is too big or in the wrong place never takes up your reading time, however busy its news page looks. Each reason to act now is set out plainly: what the page said and when, what it might mean, what cuts against it, and the question still open. A firm that fits but shows nothing current waits on the watch list with a note of what would bring it forward.

The list stays inside your company. It holds public facts, the roles at each firm and the names you supplied yourself. You decide who, if anyone, hears from you.

## Who starts the work, and when

Whoever builds the outreach list starts it: a founder, a partner, a salesperson or the marketing lead. Start when you have a segment worth approaching and no list yet, when an export of firms needs sorting before anyone writes to them, or when an older list has gone stale and needs fresh dates and grades.

That person supplies the inputs, reads the list and makes every decision about who is approached. The AI assistant prepares the list. It sends nothing, contacts no one and looks no one up.

## What they supply

- **Your facts.** A short page: what you sell, the firms you serve with their size and region, the associations and people that introduce you, and the firms never to list.
- **The request.** The kinds of firm and the role to reach in each, your must-have criteria, the signs that should move a firm up, what rules a firm out, and how many days a sign stays current.
- **The firms.** An export from the tool you already use, a typed list of names and websites, or both. The template lists the column headings it expects and stops to ask when yours differ.
- **Your notes from public pages.** The address, the date and what each page said: about pages, postings, news, an association's events.
- **Optional:** earlier lists, so duplicates and do-not-contact marks carry over, and permission for the assistant to read firms' own public pages itself.

## What comes out

1. **The search profile.** Your must-haves, your ranking signs and your rule-outs, each with where it came from, plus the questions only a conversation can answer.
2. **By firm type and role.** For each kind of firm and role, how many firms landed in each grade, the strongest one, and where that role shows up in public.
3. **The firms.** Each with its criteria marked seen, not yet seen or ruled out; each sign in four lines; the sponsor, the way in and the likely work; a grade of Ready, Watch or Not now with the rule behind it; a confidence; the next step; what would change the grade; and one sentence that sums it up.
4. **People.** Only the names you supplied, each traced to its row, and every do-not-contact mark carried forward.
5. **Not a fit, left out and unavailable.** Each with its reason, so nothing disappears quietly.
6. **A list check and the sources,** every one dated.

Good looks like this: you could explain any firm's place on the list in one breath, and point to the page that backs it.

## What has to change for your company

- **Your facts page.** Keep it current, with the do-not-contact list and the date of every mark.
- **Your criteria.** The firm types, the size measure and what your region means, plus the signs and rule-outs drawn from the clients you won and the ones that went quiet.
- **Your freshness limit.** How long a posting or an announcement counts as current for you.
- **Your export.** Match your tool's column headings to the template's once.
- **Your doors.** The associations you belong to and the people who introduce you.
- **Your rules for approaching people.** Who approves outreach, and the rules for each country you write to, settled with whoever advises you.
- **Your platform.** It is written to run in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, from what you supply. Decide whether the assistant may read public pages itself and where finished lists are kept.
