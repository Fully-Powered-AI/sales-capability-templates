# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI from the working guide it uses to build its own prospect lists. That guide adapts the prospecting method in coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT): the target profile as a short statement with pass-or-fail criteria, a source and date on every row, confidence that rises only when independent sources agree, a note of what to leave out when writing to a firm, and no bulk extraction and no sensitive traits. Keeping company fit apart from signs of change, the three states of each criterion (with the rule in the competitor-profiling method of coreyhaines31/marketingskills that not observed is not absent), the four-part reading of each signal, the single next action and the condition that would change a verdict draw on swan-gtm/gtm-skills (MIT). Testing firms on firm-level criteria before looking at anyone in them, and keeping the firms apart from the people, draw on LeadMagic/gtm-skills (MIT). Fully Powered AI added the separate next step beside the grade, the rule that names come only from a source you supply, the size and location rules, and the doors. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** The working guide Fully Powered AI uses to build its own prospect lists: names only from a supplied source with roles recorded instead, size counted at the firm that receives the work, headquarters kept apart from offices, the door as a way in that is not a cold approach, the early-adopter sign, a next step kept separate from the grade, and do-not-contact marks that are never removed.
