Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The method draws on the rfp-responder method in alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT, commercial/skills/rfp-responder): sorting a request's requirements into must-haves, scored items and preferences by the request's own wording; keeping the requirements in the buyer's order and in the buyer's words; checking each one against evidence someone could verify, as covered, part covered or a gap, and never writing around a gap; treating a must-have the company cannot meet as a threat to the whole bid; writing the bid criteria down before the request is read; weighing late entry, an incumbent and the number of rival bidders; the three outcomes of bid, bid with a partner and no bid; tying every requirement to the part of the response that answers it; and putting the most effort where the request puts the most points. Fully Powered AI's own are the split into a decision pack and a dated plan, the rule that a named person records the decision before any plan is made, criteria shown as met, not met or unknown in place of any chance-of-winning figure, buyer questions dated to the buyer's own deadline, the plan counted back in working days from the deadline with flags for missing owners, absences and people reviewing their own work, and the rule that no price or terms are set and nothing is sent or submitted. No third-party text is reused.
