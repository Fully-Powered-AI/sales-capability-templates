# Bid or no-bid and RFP response plan: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The company, the school district, the people, the request, the dates and the amounts below are all invented. Alder & Finch Mechanical is an invented 40-person heating and air-conditioning service contractor. Its bid manager received an invented request for proposals from an invented public school district. First come the inputs for part A, in full, and the decision pack the workflow template returned. Then come the extra inputs for part B, supplied the next day after the managing director had decided, and the response plan the workflow template returned.

## Part A: the inputs

### 1. The buyer's request

```
CALDER PLAINS SCHOOL DISTRICT
REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS 2026-14
HVAC PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR SERVICES

1. Overview
The District invites proposals for preventive maintenance and repair of the
heating, ventilation and air-conditioning (HVAC) equipment at the 14 sites
listed in Attachment A. The contract term is three years from 1 February
2027, with two optional one-year extensions. The District's budget for this
service is approximately $240,000 per year.

2. Timetable
Issued: Monday 2 November 2026.
Notice of intent to respond: by email to the procurement officer by
  Wednesday 11 November 2026, 5:00 pm.
Written questions: by Friday 13 November 2026, 4:00 pm, by email.
Answers published: Friday 20 November 2026.
Proposals due: Tuesday 8 December 2026, 2:00 pm Pacific time, through the
  District's procurement portal. Late proposals will not be accepted.

3. Format
One PDF file of no more than 40 pages, not counting the forms. Sections in
the order of section 6.

4. Mandatory requirements
A proposal that does not meet every requirement in this section will be
rejected. Include the evidence for 4.1 to 4.6 with Form A.
4.1 The respondent must hold a valid state HVAC contractor licence.
4.2 The respondent shall provide a certificate of insurance showing general
    liability of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence, automobile liability of
    at least $1,000,000 and workers' compensation as required by law.
4.3 Every technician performing refrigerant work shall hold EPA Section 608
    Universal certification.
4.4 The respondent shall provide emergency service 24 hours a day, 7 days a
    week, with a technician on site within 4 hours of the District's call.
4.5 Every technician assigned to District sites must pass a fingerprint-based
    background check before working on a District site.
4.6 The respondent must have provided HVAC maintenance to at least two
    public-sector or K-12 clients within the past five years.

5. Scope
5.1 The scope includes the HVAC equipment and related refrigeration at the
    sites in Attachment A.
5.2 Preventive maintenance visits shall be made quarterly at every site.
5.3 The contractor should be on site for urgent, non-emergency calls within
    24 hours.
5.4 Respondents may propose optional energy-saving recommendations in section
    6.3, priced separately on Form C.
5.5 Experience with networked building automation systems is desirable.

6. Proposal contents and scoring (100 points)
6.1 Cover letter, with Form A (Respondent Information) and Form B
    (Non-collusion Declaration), each signed by an authorized officer.
    Pass or fail.
6.2 Company qualifications and experience with comparable school facilities,
    including rooftop packaged units and hot-water boilers. 25 points.
6.3 Maintenance approach and response times, including how the respondent
    will meet 5.2 and 5.3. 25 points.
6.4 Staffing: the account manager and lead technicians, with their
    qualifications. 20 points.
6.5 References: three public-sector or K-12 clients for work within the past
    five years. 10 points.
6.6 Pricing on Form C: an annual preventive maintenance fee for each of the
    15 sites, and hourly rates for repairs. 20 points.

7. Services agreement
The successful respondent must sign the District's services agreement
(Attachment D). List any exceptions in the cover letter. Exceptions may
affect scoring.

Attachment A. Sites: nine elementary schools, three middle schools, one high
school and the District service center, all in Calder County.
Attachment C. Forms A, B and C (fillable PDF).
Attachment D. Services agreement (separate file).
```
