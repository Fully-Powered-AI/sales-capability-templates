# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The shape of the review draws on two published methods. From anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0): rolling the open pipeline up stage by stage with count, value, time in stage, missing next steps and inactivity; flagging deals whose close date has passed or moved out, that have no next step, have gone quiet or have sat in a stage too long; comparing pipeline with what remains of the target; a win rate and cycle length from recently closed deals; a short list of where to focus first, led by the largest deal at risk; and working read-only from an uploaded export. From markfulton/ai-employees (MIT): tying every figure to the file it came from and never estimating one; withholding a rate until enough cases sit behind it; counting a change as movement only when it clears both an amount and a percentage; taking last week's figures only from last week's record; and resting each call on a figure printed on the same page, never on a person. Fully Powered AI added the fixed export columns with a stop when a header does not match, the bridge from last week's open value to this week's, deals that vanish from the export held under Could not assess, blank amounts treated as unknown rather than zero, a question for each at-risk deal's owner, and a forecast line left blank for a named person to commit. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Fully Powered AI's own rules for prepared reviews: fixed export columns with a stop on any mismatch, a bridge that proves last week's open value becomes this week's, missing deals held apart rather than counted as lost, blank amounts kept as unknown, and a forecast line that only a named person fills.
