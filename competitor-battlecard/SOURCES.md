LeadMagic/gtm-skills (MIT), its battlecard-builder package, contributed setting each point out as what is true, why it matters to the deal and what to say, a structure that package itself credits to a published battlecard framework; a short answer with one follow-up question for when the competitor is mentioned in passing; a price comparison that shows what each side includes and costs extra; acknowledging the competitor's strength before the difference; and keeping prices current. Tagging every line to a dated source, the recheck date and the stale-source rule, reporting silence as no mention found, the held-back list, buyer reports kept apart from facts, nothing about a competitor's finances, staff, lawsuits or security, and the head-to-head count with its small-sample rule are Fully Powered AI's own.
