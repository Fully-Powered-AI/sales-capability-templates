# Competitor battlecard

Your salespeople get one page on one named competitor: where it is strong, where you are stronger and the proof, what to say when it comes up, and the questions to ask. Nothing about the competitor goes on the card without a dated source; anything unsupported is held back for the approver.

*Workflow template · Sales · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/competitor-battlecard/)*

## The outcome

Every salesperson walks into a competitive deal with the same honest picture of the other side. The card says what the competitor tells buyers and what it charges, then sets out where it is genuinely strong before it gets to where you win. Each of your advantages comes with the proof you are allowed to share and a question that puts the difference in front of the buyer.

Nothing about the competitor is stated as fact without a page behind it. Each line about the competitor names the page it came from and the date that page was saved, so a buyer who checks finds exactly what your salesperson said. Rumours, stale pages and figures nobody can show are listed separately for the approver and kept off the card. When a competitor really is the better choice for a kind of deal, the card says so, and your sales lead decides whether to chase it.

## Who starts the work, and when

The sales lead, a product marketer or whoever looks after sales material starts it when a competitor keeps turning up in deals, when new salespeople start meeting it, or when the card's recheck date arrives. It runs again when the competitor changes its prices or your own facts change.

The person who starts it names the competitor and supplies the evidence. The approver, usually the sales lead, reads the draft and approves it before anyone uses it, and a person shares it with the team. The AI assistant shares nothing, contacts no one and changes no record.

## What they supply

- **The competitor's pages,** saved with the date of each: homepage, pricing and product pages, or the observed lines of a [competitor profile from public pages](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/competitor-profile-from-public-pages/).
- **Your fact sheet,** with its date: what you sell, prices and what each includes, what you do not offer, and the proof you may share with prospects.
- **Notes from deals** where the competitor came up, with what buyers said in their own words.
- **An export of closed deals** that name the competitor, if you want the head-to-head count.
- **Who the card is for,** the buyers they sell to, the approver and how often the card is rechecked.

## What comes out

One card, the same shape every time:

1. **In short,** in two sentences.
2. **What they say:** their positioning, their published prices and their main claims, recorded as claims.
3. **Where they are strong,** stated plainly.
4. **Where you are stronger,** each point with its proof and a question to ask.
5. **If they come up:** what to say in a sentence or two, and the next question.
6. **What buyers have told you,** in their words, with dates.
7. **The objections they bring,** each with a short, true answer and a follow-up question.
8. **Questions that bring out the difference,** what not to raise first, and where they fit better.

Beneath the card sit the head-to-head count from your export, what changed since the last card, the lines held back and why, and every source with its age and the date to recheck by.

## What has to change for your company

- **Your fact sheet.** Give it an owner and a date. Your side of the card is built only from it, and the proof you may share is whatever it records permission for.
- **Your approver.** Name who approves a card before salespeople use it and who shares it.
- **Your recheck interval.** Ninety days by default; shorten it for a competitor that changes its prices often.
- **Your export.** Agree the columns once. If your sales system names them differently, write the mapping down; the assistant stops and asks rather than guess.
- **Which competitors get a card,** and where the approved cards live.
- **Your platform.** The instructions are written to be pasted into ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. It works from files and notes you supply and needs no connection to your sales system.
