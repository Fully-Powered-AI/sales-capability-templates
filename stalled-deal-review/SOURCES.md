# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI from its own working guide for keeping a record of sales conversations, which is itself a rewrite of the revenue operations method in coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT): sales stages that each have an entry, an exit and an owner, a deal flagged when it sits in one stage too long, and a loss reason recorded on every lost deal. Two methods in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0) shape the reading. From its deal-advance-gap method (sales/skills/deal-advance-gap): reading a stuck deal against what its current stage still lacks, and saying whether the next move belongs to the buyer, to the seller or to a colleague inside the company. From its win-loss-review method (sales/skills/win-loss-review): recording the stage each lost deal ended at, so the noes can be read stage by stage. Sorting each recorded next step by whether it has a date, has none or waits on the other party, and holding every statement in the review to the record before anyone relies on it, draw on two methods in CALLE-AI/awesome-phone-call-agents (MIT), its commitment tracker (skills/call-agent-commitment-tracker) and its summary faithfulness check (skills/call-post-summary-faithfulness-auditor). The coverage line, the questions that settle each stall, quoting the buyer's own words for each reason and the rule that nothing in the record is changed are Fully Powered AI's own. No third-party text is reused.
