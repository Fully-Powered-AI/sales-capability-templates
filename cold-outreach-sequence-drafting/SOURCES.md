The method our own guide adapted: gather the facts before choosing the angle and keep a source for each one, fall back to the segment when nothing specific to the company is found, open on the reader's situation, tie any personal detail to the problem, make one small ask, keep subject lines plain, give every follow-up something new, cut the wording that marks a note as mass-produced (stock openers, jargon, questions the note answers itself, "just checking in", several links or images, false reply markers), honour the closing note, and let any reply stop every channel, with the not-now, away, opt-out and bounce cases handled apart.
