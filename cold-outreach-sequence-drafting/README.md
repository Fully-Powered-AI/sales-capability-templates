# Cold outreach sequence drafting

Your outreach reads like you wrote it on your best day: a first note and its follow-ups, drafted in your own voice, where every line about the prospect traces to a dated fact and every line about your company traces to facts you supplied. You read it, edit it and send it yourself.

*Workflow template · Sales · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/cold-outreach-sequence-drafting/)*

## The outcome

A stranger opens your note and sees their own work in the first line, understands what you do in the second, and has one small thing to answer. The follow-ups are already written, each with something new to say, and the last one closes politely and means it.

Nothing in the sequence is made up. A line about the prospect comes from a fact you supplied, with its source and date. A line about your company comes from your own short list of facts. Where there is nothing specific to say about a company, the note speaks to the kind of firm it is and pretends to know nothing more. No invented result, no client named without permission, no countdown that does not exist.

## Who starts the work, and when

The person whose name is on the note starts it: a founder, a partner or a salesperson doing their own outreach. Start when you have a prospect or a segment worth writing to and no note yet, or a first note that needs its follow-ups.

That person reads every line, fills the slots, decides whether anything goes, and sends from their own mailbox. The AI assistant drafts. It sends nothing and contacts no one.

## What they supply

- **Your facts.** A short page: what you sell and to whom, the proof you can support, what you never say, and who signs the note.
- **The prospect or the segment.** The role and kind of company, and any facts about them, each with where it came from and its date. A line on what to leave out.
- **The ask.** The one thing you want back: a reply to a question, permission to send something to read, or a short call.
- **The sequence settings.** How many notes and how far apart, or let the assistant propose them.
- **Optional:** an email or two you really wrote, for your voice, and anything short you have for the reader to read.

## What comes out

1.  **The angle.** The facts used, with sources, kept apart from what is only a guess about why they matter.
2.  **The first note.** A plain subject line and a body short enough to read on a phone, with one ask.
3.  **The follow-ups.** Each stands alone and adds one new thing. The last says it is the last.
4.  **What ends the sequence.** Any reply stops the remaining notes; an opt-out is permanent.
5.  **The grounding.** One line per claim, pointing to the fact it rests on.
6.  **Your slots.** The lines only you can fill, and the lines to read twice before sending.

Good looks like this: you could defend every sentence if the reader asked where it came from, and the reader could answer in one line.

## What has to change for your company

- **Your facts page.** Write it once and keep it current: what you do, the proof you can stand behind, and what is off limits.
- **Your permission rule.** Which clients may be named, and who says so.
- **Your ask and your settings.** The action you want, the number of notes and the spacing.
- **Your sending rules.** Who sends, from which mailbox, and the identification and opt-out lines your recipients' countries require. You supply those lines; the workflow template leaves the slots.
- **Your platform.** It is written to run in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. Decide which files the assistant may read for this work.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
