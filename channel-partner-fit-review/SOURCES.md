# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Eight files of the partnerships method in alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT), all under commercial/skills/partnerships-architect, shaped the work. From its main instructions: building the review around the moment a would-be partner gets in touch, asking whether it brings buyers or mainly wants better terms, which type it should be, how a first quarter would prove it and what rate still pays better than selling yourself, and leaving the choice to a person; and, for selling together, asking for the shared offer in one sentence with the customer it is for, an outcome neither side could deliver alone. From assets/partnership_intake_template.md: asking for the past year's customers that you would want too, counting only people who are paid to sell, writing unknown in place of a guess, and a few warning signs, among them a hurry to sign and a region asked for outright. From references/channel_partner_canon.md: partner types that run from passing on introductions to selling together, and checking whether the customers a partner names already knew you. From references/joint_gtm_canon.md: a first ninety days with groundwork, a midpoint check and a choice at the end to carry on, change or stop, and the question of whether a reseller can sell with none of your own salespeople present. From references/partnership_anti_patterns.md: settling in advance what would end a partnership, making any marketing money pay for a specific activity with a stated return, putting conditions on any region held alone, and paying only for introductions that were new. From scripts/revshare_modeler.py: setting the margin on a deal you win yourself beside the same deal won through a partner, and counting how many partner deals a year cover the partnership's fixed cost. From scripts/partner_tier_classifier.py: placing a partner at the most demanding type it fully qualifies for, with referral to fall back on. From scripts/joint_gtm_planner.py: letting the partner's type decide which steps the plan holds. Fully Powered AI built the work around the organization's own figures as the only basis for the economics, with no typical rates supplied; the three marks for every claim and the rule that only someone other than the partner can confirm one; the check against your own accounts export with its dates; the year break-even rate at the partner's own promised volume; ninety-day targets worked out from that promise and your win rate; agreement terms set aside for the person who handles them; and a person who decides and replies. No third-party text is reused.
