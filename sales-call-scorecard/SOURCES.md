# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words, as a version that reads one call from a transcript or notes the person supplies against the team's own written criteria. Three published methods, all under the MIT licence, shaped parts of it. From swan-gtm/gtm-skills, its evidence-based call scoring method: quoting the words behind every mark with the speaker and the time, marking gaps in the recording before reading, keeping what the buyer stated apart from what the seller claimed, a criterion outside the call type marked not applicable rather than missed, showing what is missing beside each mark, one specific coaching focus tied to the next call, leaving any record change to a person, and having two reviewers mark the same calls to align the criteria. From swan-gtm/gtm-skills, its call KPI scoring method: placing every speaker on one side or the other before scoring, with a guessed placement confirmed by the person first and outside parties kept apart; checking the stated call type against what the call covered; a talk-balance figure from word counts, labelled as an estimate when there are no timestamps; and reading how the conversation went and where it left the deal as two things, saying so when they disagree. From Zoominfo/zoominfo-mcp-plugin, its call coaching method: a short list of what went well and what to improve, each tied to a moment in the call and each improvement paired with something to try instead, working from notes when there is no recording, and declining to coach on a point the material is too sparse to support. Fully Powered AI's own: the team's criteria as the only yardstick, the five marks including one for what the material cannot settle, the rule that silence in notes never counts as a miss, no talk balance worked out from notes, notes and tool summaries never quoted as the buyer's words, the separate record of what the buyer said, the checks on the call's goal and the last focus, no overall score and no use in any decision about employment, and personal details left out. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Its own method for reviewing one sales call for coaching: the team's written criteria as the only yardstick, five marks including one for what the material cannot settle, silence in notes never counted as a miss, the buyer's own words kept apart from the seller's, the goal and last-focus checks, and no score, rating or use in employment decisions.
