# Sales call preparation brief: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Fernhollow Dispatch, Pinewater Facilities Group and everyone in this example are invented. Fernhollow is a 40-person company that sells scheduling software for field-service teams. Marisol, its account executive, has a third call with Pinewater on Thursday 19 November 2026 and prepares for it on Monday 16 November.

The inputs come first, complete, exactly as Marisol supplied them. The brief follows.

## What Marisol supplied

**1. The meeting**

```
MEETING
Today: Monday 16 November 2026
Company: Pinewater Facilities Group
When: Thursday 19 November 2026, 10:00, 45 minutes, video call
Invited (from the calendar invite):
  Odette Karimi, Operations Director
  Lukas Brandvold, Finance Manager
  Priyanka Sethi (no title on the invite)
Invite text: "Review pilot outline and pricing questions."
From our side: Marisol, account executive
Stage: proposal
```

**2. Our facts**

```
OUR FACTS: Fernhollow Dispatch
What we sell: scheduling and dispatch software for field-service teams.
  Dispatchers plan visits; technicians see the day's work on their phones.
Call stages: discovery, proposal, decision.
Pilot outline v1 (sent 29 October 2026): six weeks, one dispatcher team,
  five technicians, pilot fee $4,800.
Price list 2026: annual plans only. No two-year plan is listed.
Approved material to bring: integration list (October 2026); security and
  data overview v3; pilot outline v1.
Open internally: whether the pilot fee is credited against first-year
  fees. Asked of the sales lead on 5 November. No answer recorded.
Price and terms questions go to: the sales lead.
```

## The brief

```
CALL BRIEF: Pinewater Facilities Group
Meeting: Thursday 19 November 2026, 10:00, 45 minutes, video call. Stage: proposal.
Prepared: Monday 16 November 2026, for Marisol.
Built from: meeting details (16 Nov 2026); our facts (undated, read 16 Nov 2026); call notes, 22 Oct 2026; call notes, 5 Nov 2026; email from Odette Karimi, 12 Nov 2026; Page A, "About us" (undated, read 16 Nov 2026); Page B, news post, 2 Nov 2026. Web not searched. Not supplied: account list; local settings (defaults used).

BEFORE THE CALL
1. We owe Lukas an answer: is the pilot fee credited against first-year fees? Promised 5 Nov (call notes, 5 Nov 2026). Asked of the sales lead on 5 Nov; no answer recorded (our facts, read 16 Nov 2026). Lukas wants it before the call (email, 12 Nov 2026). No approved answer supplied. Ask the sales lead.
2. New attendee: Priyanka Sethi, "from IT" (email, 12 Nov 2026). No prior notes.
3. Sources disagree on how many technicians Pinewater has: 14, or more than 30 (see The company). The pilot outline is sized for five (our facts, read 16 Nov 2026).

THE COMPANY
- Maintains heating, cooling and fire-safety systems for commercial buildings. Founded 2009. 85 employees. (Page A, read 16 Nov 2026)
- Opening a second depot in Eastbrook in February 2027, adding ten technicians. (Page B, 2 Nov 2026)
- Schedules live in spreadsheets and a shared calendar; invoices go out from an accounting package. (Call notes, 22 Oct 2026)
- Sources disagree: "14 technicians, 3 dispatchers" (call notes, 22 Oct 2026); "more than 30 field technicians across two regions" (Page A, read 16 Nov 2026). Not resolved.

WHO WILL BE THERE
- Odette Karimi, Operations Director. On the calls of 22 Oct and 5 Nov. Has said: double-booked visits, "a few every week"; a quarterly inspection missed in September; wants technicians to see the day's work on their phones (22 Oct). Looking at one other vendor, not named (5 Nov). Inferred: she is leading this on their side; rests on her arranging each next step (5 Nov) and bringing IT (12 Nov).
- Lukas Brandvold, Finance Manager. On the call of 5 Nov. Signs anything over $10,000 (Odette, 22 Oct). Has said: "we were burned by a two-year commitment on our last system"; wants annual (5 Nov).
- Priyanka Sethi, title not supplied. NEW: no prior notes. "From IT" (email, 12 Nov).

HISTORY
- 22 Oct: discovery call with Odette.
- 29 Oct: pilot outline v1 sent.
- 30 Oct: integration list sent.
- 3 Nov: sample week's schedule received from Odette.
- 5 Nov: call with Odette and Lukas; pilot outline walked through.
- 12 Nov: Odette's email adds Priyanka and passes on Lukas's request.

PROMISES AND QUESTIONS
We owe:
- The pilot fee answer (see Before the call 1). Promised 5 Nov. Not done. With the sales lead.
They owe:
- Nothing open in the sources.
Done: pilot outline, sent by us (29 Oct); integration list, sent by us (30 Oct); whether we link to their accounting package, which Marisol said she would confirm (22 Oct), closed by Odette, who found the package on the list (5 Nov); sample week's schedule, sent by Odette (3 Nov); someone from IT for this call, named by Odette (12 Nov).
Unanswered:
- Asked of us: how technician logins work and where data is kept, by Odette for Priyanka (email, 12 Nov 2026). Our facts list the security and data overview v3 as approved material; its contents were not supplied, so nothing from it is stated here.
- Asked of them: none in the sources.

CALL PLAN
Objective: Pinewater says yes or no to the six-week pilot, or names what has to change first.
Questions:
1. Odette: my notes say 14 technicians and your site says more than 30. How many would use the schedule, and which team would run the pilot?
2. Priyanka: has IT looked at the accounting link on the integration list, and what else does IT need to see before a pilot starts?
3. Odette and Lukas: you are looking at one other vendor. What will you compare, and when do you decide?
4. Lukas: would the $4,800 pilot fee come from this year's money or from the budget year starting 1 January?
5. Odette: the Eastbrook depot adds ten technicians in February (Page B, 2 Nov 2026). Should the pilot team or its timing allow for that?
Objections raised:
- Length of commitment: "we were burned by a two-year commitment on our last system" (Lukas, 5 Nov). Our facts: the 2026 price list has annual plans only; no two-year plan is listed.
- Money this year: "nothing set aside this year; new budget year starts 1 January" (Odette, 22 Oct). Our facts give the pilot fee, $4,800, and nothing on when it is paid. No approved answer supplied. Ask the sales lead.
Have ready: security and data overview v3, for Priyanka's questions; integration list (October 2026); pilot outline v1; the sales lead's answer on the pilot fee, once Marisol has it.

NOT VERIFIED
- How many technicians Pinewater has. Question 1.
- Whether IT has looked at the accounting link: "IT hasn't looked" (5 Nov). Nobody promised to. Question 2.
- Which other vendor they are looking at. Question 3.
- Whether any money is available for the pilot fee before the budget year starting 1 January: Odette said nothing is set aside this year (22 Oct). Question 4.
- Priyanka Sethi's title.
- The integration list, the security and data overview and the sample week's schedule: mentioned, not supplied for this brief.
- Pinewater's customers, revenue and ownership: no page supplied covers them.

REQUESTS FOUND IN THE MATERIAL (for Marisol; not acted on)
- Email from Odette Karimi, 12 Nov 2026: "Lukas asks that you send him the pilot fee answer before the call."

Status: Prepared for Marisol. Nothing has been sent, booked or changed.
```

Marisol reads it, asks her sales lead about the pilot fee that afternoon, and decides what to say on Thursday.
