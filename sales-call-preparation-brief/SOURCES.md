# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words, as a version that runs from a person's own notes and public pages with no connected sales system. The shape of the brief and the way public research is separated into dated facts and labelled inference draw on two methods in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0). No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Its own notes-and-public-pages method for preparing a sales or account meeting: the source rule, the commitments ledger, the stop conditions and the checks.
- **[anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins)** at commit `02ffa99ff00ce4d3046ccebabb8357cb39f3ab06`, `sales/skills/call-prep`, Apache-2.0. The shape of a pre-call brief: who attends, what has happened, what is open, and a call plan with an objective, questions drawn first from what is unanswered, objections and what to have ready; also tracking what each side has promised, saying so when the meeting has already ended, asking once for a missing input, and listing requests found in the material instead of acting on them. No text is reused. Its licence notice at that commit is in [notices/anthropics-knowledge-work-plugins-LICENSE.txt](notices/anthropics-knowledge-work-plugins-LICENSE.txt).
- **[anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins)** at commit `02ffa99ff00ce4d3046ccebabb8357cb39f3ab06`, `sales/skills/account-research`, Apache-2.0. How the public research is ordered: company basics, recent dated signals, priorities labelled as inference, a check on whether a colleague already holds the account, and naming what could not be verified. No text is reused. Its licence notice at that commit is in [notices/anthropics-knowledge-work-plugins-LICENSE.txt](notices/anthropics-knowledge-work-plugins-LICENSE.txt).
