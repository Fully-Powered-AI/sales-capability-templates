# Sales proposal preparation

Your account executive answers an RFP in minutes, not weeks, with a proposal draft where every claim traces to approved company information and every gap is already a question for the right person.

*Workflow template · Sales · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/sales-proposal-preparation/)*

## The outcome

The buyer’s request arrives and the proposal pack is ready the same day: an account brief, a draft in the buyer’s format, an evidence checklist and the open questions. Your reviewers check sources instead of rewriting paragraphs.

The draft says only what your approved material supports. A requirement the company cannot evidence becomes a question. Fees and terms are copied from the approved pricing. The workflow template never invents a reference, a result or a price, and a named reviewer confirms commercial terms and claims before anything is sent.

## Who starts the work, and when

The account executive starts it when a buyer’s request arrives: an RFP, a security or procurement questionnaire, or a call that ends with “send us a proposal”. It runs again whenever the buyer answers or the scope changes.

The solutions lead confirms scope and service claims. The finance or contracts reviewer confirms fees, payment terms and contract length. The sales lead confirms references, approves submission and sends the proposal. The AI assistant sends nothing.

## What they supply

- **The buyer’s stated requirements.** The RFP or questionnaire, call notes and emails: what the buyer said or wrote.
- **Approved company information.** Service descriptions, the security overview, case summaries and references with permission on record.
- **The agreed scope.** The approved scope note and the approved pricing.
- **The response format.** The buyer’s format, or your proposal template.

## What comes out

1.  **An account brief.** The buyer, what they asked for, who decides, the agreed scope and what is open, on one page.
2.  **A proposal draft.** Every requirement answered in the buyer’s order, with held items marked for review.
3.  **An evidence checklist.** One row per claim: the claim, its approved source, its status and the role that checks it.
4.  **Open questions.** Questions for the buyer, and questions for each internal reviewer by role.

Good looks like this: a reviewer can check any sentence against its source in under a minute, and nothing surprises the finance or contracts reviewer.

## What has to change for your company

- **One home for approved material.** Each item with a version and an owner.
- **A reference register.** Which customers may be named, to whom, and when permission was recorded.
- **Your pricing authority.** The document fees are copied from, and which requested changes go to the finance or contracts reviewer.
- **Named reviewers.** Who checks scope, claims, terms and submission, by role.
- **Your platform.** It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. Decide which files the assistant may read for this work.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
