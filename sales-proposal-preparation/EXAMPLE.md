# Sales proposal preparation: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* An invented 80-person managed IT services company answers RFP 26-117 from Buyer A, an invented 300-person professional-services firm.

```
RFP 26-117, section 6.2 (input)
Provide your most recent independent security attestation report.

Security overview SO-1 (approved company information)
Readiness assessment completed 28 August 2026. No report issued.
```

**Proposal pack: Buyer A, RFP 26-117**

- **Draft:** A response to every requirement in RFP sections 3 to 6. Eight claims on the evidence checklist: five ready, one copied from PS-117, two held for review.
- **Draft response, section 6.2:** “We completed a readiness assessment for an independent security attestation report on 28 August 2026. The report has not yet been issued. We will confirm in writing the date by which we will provide it: \[date to be confirmed by the solutions lead\].”
- **Held claims:** Independent security attestation report available (SO-1: no report issued). On-site within four hours at all four offices (coverage map CM-1 covers two).
- **Commercial terms:** Copied from pricing schedule PS-117: per-device monthly fees, net 30, 24-month term. The RFP asks for net 45.
- **Questions for Buyer A:** Is a completed readiness assessment with a written report date acceptable? Is next-business-day on-site support acceptable at the two outer offices? Who is the accounts payable contact?
- **Questions for reviewers:** Solutions lead: what report date can we commit to? Sales lead: bid with the gap disclosed or decline section 6.2? Finance reviewer: net 30 or net 45?

Prepared for review. Not sent.
